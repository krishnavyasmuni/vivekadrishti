“When You wander through the forest by day, even the smallest instant becomes an age to us who cannot see You. Yet when we gaze upon Your beautiful face, framed by curling locks, the maker of eyelids seems a fool—for even a blink obstructs our sight of You.”

— Śrīmad Bhāgavatam 10.31.15, Gopī-gītā
