BP 1.9.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
एवं स्त्रीषु मनुष्याणां वृत्तिरुक्ता समासतः ।
साम्प्रतं च मनुष्येषु स्त्रीणां समुपदिश्यते । । १

Brahmā said: ‘The conduct of men toward women has thus been stated in summary; now the conduct of women toward men is taught.’

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
एवम् (evam) — thus;
स्त्रीषु मनुष्याणाम् वृत्तिः (strīṣu manuṣyāṇām vṛttiḥ) — conduct of people toward women;
उक्ता समासतः (uktā samāsataḥ) — stated in summary;
साम्प्रतम् (sāmpratam) — now;
मनुष्येषु स्त्रीणाम् (manuṣyeṣu strīṇām) — of women toward men;
समुपदिश्यते (samupadiśyate) — is taught
Transliteration
brahmovāca
evaṃ strīṣu manuṣyāṇāṃ vṛttiruktā samāsataḥ |
sāmprataṃ ca manuṣyeṣu strīṇāṃ samupadiśyate | | 1

BP 1.9.2

सम्यगाराधनात्पुंसां रतिर्वृत्तिश्च योषितः ।
पुत्राः स्वर्गाद्यदृष्टं च तस्मादिष्टो हि तद्विधिः । । २

‘Through proper mutual attentiveness come men’s affection, women’s security and way of life, sons, and unseen fruits such as heaven; therefore the relevant procedure is held desirable.’

Word-for-word
सम्यक् आराधनात् (samyak ārādhanāt) — through proper attentiveness;
पुंसाम् रतिः (puṃsām ratiḥ) — men’s affection;
वृत्तिः च योषितः (vṛttiḥ ca yoṣitaḥ) — women’s way of life;
पुत्राः (putrāḥ) — sons;
स्वर्ग-आदि अदृष्टम् च (svarga-ādi adṛṣṭam ca) — unseen fruit such as heaven;
तस्मात् इष्टः (tasmāt iṣṭaḥ) — therefore desirable;
तत्-विधिः (tat-vidhiḥ) — that procedure
Transliteration
samyagārādhanātpuṃsāṃ ratirvṛttiśca yoṣitaḥ |
putrāḥ svargādyadṛṣṭaṃ ca tasmādiṣṭo hi tadvidhiḥ | | 2

BP 1.9.3

कर्तव्यं नाम यत्किञ्चित्सर्वं १ विधिमपेक्षते ।
व्यक्तिमायाति वैफल्यं तदेवारब्धमन्यथा । । ३

‘Whatever is to be done depends upon a proper procedure. The very undertaking begun otherwise comes visibly to failure.’

Word-for-word
कर्तव्यम् नाम यत् किञ्चित् (kartavyam nāma yat kiñcit) — whatever is to be done;
सर्वम् विधिम् अपेक्षते (sarvam vidhim apekṣate) — all depends on procedure;
व्यक्तिम् आयाति वैफल्यम् (vyaktim āyāti vaiphalyam) — visibly comes to failure;
तत् एव आरब्धम् (tat eva ārabdham) — that very undertaking begun;
अन्यथा (anyathā) — otherwise
Transliteration

BP 1.9.4

विध्यपेक्षीणि सर्वाणि कार्याण्यविफलान्यपि ।
हेतुभूतास्त्रिवर्गस्य महारम्भा विशेषतः । ।४

‘All undertakings, even those otherwise capable of success, depend upon procedure—especially great undertakings that serve as causes of the three aims of life.’

Word-for-word
विधि-अपेक्षीणि (vidhi-apekṣīṇi) — dependent on procedure;
सर्वाणि कार्याणि (sarvāṇi kāryāṇi) — all undertakings;
अविफलानि अपि (aviphalāni api) — even otherwise successful;
हेतु-भूताः त्रि-वर्गस्य (hetu-bhūtāḥ tri-vargasya) — causes of three aims;
महा-आरम्भाः विशेषतः (mahā-ārambhāḥ viśeṣataḥ) — especially great undertakings
Transliteration
vidhyapekṣīṇi sarvāṇi kāryāṇyaviphalānyapi |

BP 1.9.5

सर्वसाध्याविधिज्ञानमागमैकनिबन्धनम् ।
साध्यं दृष्टमदृष्टं च द्वयं विधिनिषेधयोः । । ५

‘Knowledge of the procedure for accomplishing every end rests solely upon received scripture. Both visible and unseen results are objects of injunctions and prohibitions.’

Word-for-word
सर्व-साध्य-विधि-ज्ञानम् (sarva-sādhya-vidhi-jñānam) — knowledge of procedure for every end;
आगम-एक-निबन्धनम् (āgama-eka-nibandhanam) — rests solely on received scripture;
साध्यम् दृष्टम् अदृष्टम् च (sādhyam dṛṣṭam adṛṣṭam ca) — visible and unseen result;
द्वयम् (dvayam) — both;
विधि-निषेधयोः (vidhi-niṣedhayoḥ) — of injunction and prohibition
Transliteration
sarvasādhyāvidhijñānamāgamaikanibandhanam |
sādhyaṃ dṛṣṭamadṛṣṭaṃ ca dvayaṃ vidhiniṣedhayoḥ | | 5

BP 1.9.6

शास्त्राधिकारो न स्त्रीणां न ग्रन्थानां च धारणे ।
तस्मादिहान्ये मन्यन्ते तच्छासनमनर्थकम् । । ६

‘The source states that women do not possess independent authority to interpret the śāstras or preserve their texts; some therefore regard direct scriptural instruction addressed to them as ineffective.’

Word-for-word
शास्त्र-अधिकारः न स्त्रीणाम् (śāstra-adhikāraḥ na strīṇām) — no independent scriptural authority for women;
न ग्रन्थानाम् च धारणे (na granthānām ca dhāraṇe) — nor in retaining texts;
तस्मात् इह (tasmāt iha) — therefore here;
अन्ये मन्यन्ते (anye manyante) — some think;
तत्-शासनम् अनर्थकम् (tat-śāsanam anarthakam) — that instruction ineffective
Transliteration
śāstrādhikāro na strīṇāṃ na granthānāṃ ca dhāraṇe |
tasmādihānye manyante tacchāsanamanarthakam | | 6

BP 1.9.7

आगमैकक्रियायोगे स्त्रीणामध्यधिकारिता ।
मृते भर्तरि साध्वी स्यादित्यादौ स्मृतिभाषितम् । । ७

‘Yet women possess eligibility to perform rites grounded in received tradition; teachings in the Smṛti such as “when her husband has died, she should remain faithful” exemplify this.’

Word-for-word
आगम-एक-क्रिया-योगे (āgama-eka-kriyā-yoge) — in performance grounded in scripture;
स्त्रीणाम् अपि अधिकारिता (strīṇām api adhikāritā) — women also have eligibility;
मृते भर्तरि (mṛte bhartari) — when husband has died;
साध्वी स्यात् (sādhvī syāt) — she should remain faithful;
इति-आदौ (iti-ādau) — in statements such as this;
स्मृति-भाषितम् (smṛti-bhāṣitam) — spoken in Smṛti
Transliteration
āgamaikakriyāyoge strīṇāmadhyadhikāritā |
mṛte bhartari sādhvī syādityādau smṛtibhāṣitam | | 7

BP 1.9.8

तस्मात्कार्यमकार्यं वा विज्ञाय प्रभुरागमात् ।
गुणदोषेषु ताः सम्यक्छास्ति राजा प्रजा इव । । ८

‘Therefore, after learning from scripture what should and should not be done, the household’s responsible authority should instruct them properly concerning merits and faults, as a king instructs his subjects.’

Word-for-word
तस्मात् (tasmāt) — therefore;
कार्यम् अकार्यम् वा (kāryam akāryam vā) — what should or should not be done;
विज्ञाय आगमात् (vijñāya āgamāt) — learning from scripture;
प्रभुः (prabhuḥ) — responsible authority;
गुण-दोषेषु (guṇa-doṣeṣu) — regarding merits and faults;
ताः सम्यक् शास्ति (tāḥ samyak śāsti) — instructs them properly;
राजा प्रजाः इव (rājā prajāḥ iva) — like king his subjects
Transliteration
tasmātkāryamakāryaṃ vā vijñāya prabhurāgamāt |
guṇadoṣeṣu tāḥ samyakchāsti rājā prajā iva | | 8

BP 1.9.9

सत्येव प्रमदाः काश्चिद्विशेषाधिगतागमाः ।
यत्तु शास्त्राधिकारित्वं वचनं स्यान्निरर्थकम् । । ९

‘Some women certainly acquire exceptional knowledge of tradition; even so, the text distinguishes such knowledge from formal authority over the śāstras, otherwise its rule about eligibility would lose its intended meaning.’

Word-for-word
सन्ति एव प्रमदाः काश्चित् (santi eva pramadāḥ kāścit) — some women certainly exist;
विशेष-अधिगत-आगमाः (viśeṣa-adhigata-āgamāḥ) — who acquired exceptional tradition;
यत् तु (yat tu) — but as to;
शास्त्र-अधिकारित्वम् (śāstra-adhikāritvam) — formal scriptural authority;
वचनम् स्यात् निरर्थकम् (vacanam syāt nirarthakam) — statement would become meaningless
Transliteration
satyeva pramadāḥ kāścidviśeṣādhigatāgamāḥ |
yattu śāstrādhikāritvaṃ vacanaṃ syānnirarthakam | | 9

BP 1.9.10

केचिद्वेदविदो विप्राः कृत्यैर्वेषक्रियापराः ।
तथापि ४ जातिमात्रेण त एवात्राधिकारिणः । । 1.9.१०

‘Some brāhmaṇas who know the Veda may be occupied with unsuitable acts, disguises, or performances; nevertheless, the source says formal eligibility belongs to them here by birth-class alone.’

Word-for-word
केचित् वेद-विदः विप्राः (kecit veda-vidaḥ viprāḥ) — some Veda-knowing brāhmaṇas;
कृत्यैः वेष-क्रिया-पराः (kṛtyaiḥ veṣa-kriyā-parāḥ) — engaged in acts and disguises;
तथा अपि (tathā api) — nevertheless;
जाति-मात्रेण (jāti-mātreṇa) — by birth alone;
ते एव अत्र अधिकारिणः (te eva atra adhikāriṇaḥ) — they are eligible here
Transliteration
kecidvedavido viprāḥ kṛtyairveṣakriyāparāḥ |

BP 1.9.11

क्रियन्ते वेदशास्त्रज्ञैः प्रयोगाः शास्त्रलौकिकाः ।
स्थितमेषामदूरेऽपि शास्त्रमेव निबन्धनम् । । ११

‘Both scripturally prescribed and customary rites are carried out by those who know the Vedic śāstras; even where the text itself is not physically near, scripture remains their foundation.’

Word-for-word
क्रियन्ते (kriyante) — are performed;
वेद-शास्त्र-ज्ञैः (veda-śāstra-jñaiḥ) — by knowers of Vedic scripture;
प्रयोगाः (prayogāḥ) — rites;
शास्त्र-लौकिकाः (śāstra-laukikāḥ) — scriptural and customary;
स्थितम् एषाम् अदूरे अपि (sthitam eṣām adūre api) — even when not situated nearby;
शास्त्रम् एव निबन्धनम् (śāstram eva nibandhanam) — scripture alone is foundation
Transliteration
kriyante vedaśāstrajñaiḥ prayogāḥ śāstralaukikāḥ |
sthitameṣāmadūre'pi śāstrameva nibandhanam | | 11

BP 1.9.12

व्याधधीवरगोपालप्रभृतीनां च दृश्यते ।

सौर-आदि-दिनानाम् (saura-ādi-dinānām) — solar and other days;

BP 1.9.13

गम्यागम्यादिकार्येयं नियताचारसंस्थितिः ।
लोकानां शास्त्रवाक्यानां प्राणाः स्वेष्टनिबन्धनाः ।। १३

लोकानाम् (lokānām) — of peoples;
शास्त्र-वाक्यानाम् (śāstra-vākyānām) — of scriptural statements;
प्राणाः (prāṇāḥ) — vital principle;
स्व-इष्ट-निबन्धनाः (sva-iṣṭa-nibandhanāḥ) — bound to their desired end

BP 1.9.14

तस्माच्चतुर्णां वर्णानामाश्रमाणां च सर्वशः ।

BP 1.9.15

पौर्वापर्यं तु विज्ञातुमशक्यं लोकशास्त्रयोः ।
तच्छास्त्रमेव मन्तव्यं यथा कर्मशरीरवत् । । १५

‘It is impossible to determine temporal priority between customary life and scripture; scripture should therefore be regarded as inseparably related to practice, like action and the body through which it is done.’

तत् शास्त्रम् एव मन्तव्यम् (tat śāstram eva mantavyam) — scripture thus to be regarded;

BP 1.9.16

आगमे च पुराणे च द्विधैव नास्तिकग्रहम् ।
मार्गं महद्भिराचीर्णं प्रपद्येताविकल्पधीः । । १६

‘In both sacred tradition and Purāṇa, a twofold denial should not be seized upon as grounds for unbelief. With a mind free from indecision, one should follow the path trodden by the great.’

मार्गम् महद्भिः आचर्णम् (mārgam mahadbhiḥ ācarṇam) — path practised by great ones;
प्रपद्येत (prapadyeta) — should follow;
अविकल्प-धीः (avikalpa-dhīḥ) — with mind free from indecision

BP 1.9.17

भर्तुः आराधने विधिम् (bhartuḥ ārādhane vidhim) — procedure for attentiveness to husband
