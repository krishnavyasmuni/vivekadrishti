  • Chapter 30 — Further Worship and Rites of Gaṇeśa

BP 1.30.1

सुमन्तरुवाच
निम्बमयमङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा नित्यधूपगन्धादिभिरर्चयित्वा प्रच्छन्नं शिरसि बद्ध्वा गच्छेत् । ।
सर्वजनप्रियो भवति । श्वेतार्कमूलाङ्गुष्ठमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा धूपादिभिरर्चयित्वा सर्वान्वर्णान्वशमानयति ।।
श्वेतचन्दनमङ्गुष्ठमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा पुष्पगन्धादिभिरर्चयित्वा शुक्लचतुर्थ्यामष्टम्यां वा बलिं कुर्यादष्टसहस्रं जुहुयाद्दध्ना पायसेन राजानं वशमानयति ।
रक्तचन्दनमयं गणपतिमङ्गुष्ठमात्रं कृत्वा भौतिकं बलिं दद्याद्दधिमधुघृताहुतीनां गणपतिमष्टसहस्रं जुहुयादात्मप्रापिकां प्रजां वशमानयति । रक्तकरवीरमूलाङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कारयेत् । ।
रक्तपुष्पगन्धोपहारैर्बलिं दद्यात् । तिललवणघृतेनाष्टसहस्रं जुहुयात् । दशग्रामान्वशमानयति । ।
श्वेतकरवीराङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा तिलपिष्टदधिघृतक्षीरहरिद्रामिश्रेणाष्टसहस्रं
जुहुयाद्वेश्यां वशमानयति । ।
अश्वत्थमूलाङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा गन्धपुष्पधूपबलिं दत्त्वा शतं जुहुयाच्छत्रुं वशमानयति । ।
अर्कमूलाङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा गन्धपुष्पधूपबलीन् दद्यात् ।
तिन्दुकाष्टशतं जुहुयाच्छत्रुं वशमानयति । ।
बिल्वमूलमयमङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कृत्वा गन्धपुष्पधूपार्चितं कृत्वा त्रिमध्वक्तानामष्टसहस्रं जुहुयाद्राजामात्यान्वशमानयति । ।
शिरसि धूपान्धृत्वा गच्छेद्राजद्वारं विग्रहे जयो भवति ।
हस्तिदन्तमृत्तिकामयमङ्गुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कारयेत् । ।
गन्धपुष्पधूपार्चितं कृत्वा. कृष्णचतुर्थ्यां नग्नो भूत्वाभ्यर्चयेत् ।
सप्त वाराञ्जपेन्नित्यं १ नारीणां सुभगो भवति । ।

Sumantu said: Fashion a thumb-joint-sized Gaṇapati from neem, worship it daily with incense, fragrance, and other offerings, bind it secretly upon the head, and go forth; one becomes dear to everyone. A Gaṇapati of white arka root, similarly worshipped, is said to bring all social groups under one’s influence. A thumb-sized image of white sandalwood, worshipped with flowers and fragrances, is offered bali on the bright fourth or eighth lunar day; eight thousand oblations of curds and milk-rice are said to influence a king. A red-sandalwood image is given a material offering and eight thousand oblations of curds, honey, and ghee, said to influence one’s subjects. A red-oleander-root image is offered red flowers, fragrances, sesame, salt, and ghee; eight thousand oblations are said to influence ten villages. A white-oleander image receives a mixture of sesame paste, curds, ghee, milk, and turmeric in eight thousand oblations, said to influence a courtesan. A sacred-fig-root image, worshipped with fragrance, flowers, incense, and bali, receives one hundred oblations to influence an enemy. An arka-root image receives the same worship and eight hundred tinduka offerings for that purpose. A bilva-root image, worshipped and offered eight thousand portions anointed with the three sweet substances, is said to influence a king and his ministers. Carrying the incense upon one’s head and approaching the royal gate is said to bring victory in a dispute. One should also fashion a thumb-joint-sized Gaṇapati from earth associated with elephant ivory, worship it with fragrance, flowers, and incense, and worship unclothed on the dark fourth; seven daily recitations are said to make one attractive to women.

BP 1.30.2

वृषभशृङ्गमृत्तिकाङ्गुष्ठमात्रं गणपतिं कारयेत् ।
गन्धपुष्पार्चितं कृत्वा गुग्गुलुधूपं दद्याद्घोषपतिं वशमानयति । ।
अथ वा वल्मीकमृत्तिकांगुष्ठपर्वमात्रं गणपतिं कारयेत् ।
कटुकतैलेन प्रतिमां लेपयेत् । ।
उन्मत्तककाष्ठेनाग्निं प्रज्वाल्याहुतीनामष्टसहस्रं जुहुयात्तिलसर्षपमिश्रेण सर्वधूपं दद्यात्त्रिकटुकेन लेपयेत् । ।
अगरुधूपं दद्याद्राजानं वशमानयति । परेषां च वल्लभो भवति । रक्तचन्दनेनात्मानं धूपयेत्सुभगो भवति । ।
ॐ गणपतये वक्रतुण्डाय गजदन्ताय गुलगुलेतिनिनादाय२ चतुर्भुजाय त्रिनेत्राय मुशलपाशवज्रहस्ताय सर्वभूतदमनाय सर्वलोकवशंकराय सर्वदुष्टोपघातजननाय सर्वशत्रुविमर्दनाय सर्वराज्यसमीहनाय राजानमिह वशमानय हन हन पच पच वज्राङ्कुशेन गणेश फट् स्वाहा ।

One should fashion a thumb-sized Gaṇapati from earth taken at a bull’s horn, worship it with fragrance and flowers, and offer guggulu incense; the text says this influences the head of a settlement. Alternatively, fashion one from anthill earth and anoint it with pungent oil. Kindle fire with dhattūra wood and offer eight thousand oblations mixed with sesame and mustard, fumigate it completely, anoint it with the three pungent spices, and offer agarwood incense; this is said to influence a king and make one beloved by others. Fumigating oneself with red sandalwood is said to make one attractive. The passage then gives a long invocation to four-armed, three-eyed, elephant-tusked Vakratuṇḍa, who bears pestle, noose, and thunderbolt, asking Gaṇeśa to subdue beings, overcome enemies, obtain sovereignty, and bring the king under influence.

The text sets out the seed syllables ‘oṃ gāṃ gīṃ gūṃ gaiṃ gauṃ gaḥ,’ followed by the heart, head, topknot, face, eyes, armour, invocation, and weapon formulas. It names the mantras for invocation, dismissal, fragrance, and flowers, and assigns Durgaya in the east, Dhūrjaṭi in the south, Lambodara in the west, Gaṇapati in the north, Gaṇādhipati in the northeast, Mahāgaṇapati in the southeast, Kūṣmāṇḍa in the southwest, and three-eyed Ekadanta Tripuraghātin in the northwest. The Gāyatrī reads: ‘We know Mahāgaṇapati; we meditate upon Vakratuṇḍa; may the Tusked One inspire us.’ After displaying eight gestures—lotus, tusk, rosary, drawing gesture, axe, goad, noose, and drum—one performs the rites. Eight thousand black-sesame oblations are said to influence a king. Eleven gestures from invocation through food offering are shown in order. O king, Vighneśa Gaṇapati should be worshipped by the same method used for three-eyed, trident-bearing Hara; the diagram and placement of limbs are likewise the same, except for the mantra words. Whoever worships one-tusked Vighneśa, Umā’s son, has obstacles destroyed and never suffers misfortune.

कर्णलेपैस्तुण्डिकाभिर्मोदकैश्च१ महीपते । ।
पूजयेत्सततं देवं विघ्नविनाशाय दन्तिनम् । । ९

Therefore, O king, one should always worship the Tusked God with devotion foremost, using ear-shaped cakes, trunk-shaped sweets, and modakas, for the destruction of obstacles.
