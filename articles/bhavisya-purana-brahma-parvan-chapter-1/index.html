Bhaviṣya Purāṇa — Brahma-parvan — Chapter 1

Kathāprastāvanā — The opening of the narration. Sanskrit text is given in Devanāgarī, followed by IAST transliteration, concise word meanings, and a literal English translation. Commentary is intentionally omitted.

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa 1.1.1

Sanskrit

जयति भुवनदीपो भास्करो लोककर्त्ता,
जयति च शितिदेहः शार्ङ्गधन्वा मुरारिः ।
जयति च शशिमौली रुद्रनामाभिधेयो,
जयति च स तु देवो भानुमांश्चित्रभानुः ॥ १ ॥

Transliteration

jayati bhuvanadīpo bhāskaro lokakarttā | jayati ca śitidehaḥ śārṅgadhanvā murāriḥ | jayati ca śaśimaulī rudranāmābhidheyo | jayati ca sa tu devo bhānumāṃścitrabhānuḥ || 1 ||

Synonyms

jayati — triumphs; bhuvana-dīpaḥ — lamp of the worlds; bhāskaraḥ — the Sun; loka-karttā — maker of the world; śārṅga-dhanvā murāriḥ — Murāri who bears the Śārṅga bow; śaśi-maulī — the moon-crested one; rudra — Rudra; bhānumān, citrabhānuḥ — the radiant, wondrously shining Sun.

Translation

Victorious is Bhāskara, the lamp of the worlds and maker of the world; victorious is Murāri, fair-bodied and bearing the Śārṅga bow. Victorious is the moon-crested deity called Rudra, and victorious is the radiant god, the wondrously shining Sun.

Transliteration

Translation

All the great-souled sages came to see the mighty King Śatānīka, who was surrounded by royal prosperity.

Among them were Bhṛgu, Atri, Vasiṣṭha, Pulastya, Pulaha, Kratu, Parāśara, Vyāsa, Sumantu, and Jaimini.

There were also the sage Paila, Yājñavalkya, the greatly austere Gautama, the wise Bhāradvāja, and the sages Nārada and Parvata.

Also present were the great-souled Vaiśampāyana, the greatly austere Śaunaka, Dakṣa, Aṅgiras, Garga, and the greatly austere Gālava.

Seeing that the sages had arrived, the wise King Śatānīka approached them and honoured them according to proper rite.

With his priest at the forefront, he honoured them all with arghya, a cow, and words of welcome, and bowed deeply with his head.

Transliteration

Translation

When they were comfortably seated, undisturbed and rested, the king bowed, raised his right arm, and addressed them.

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa 1.1.9

Sanskrit

इदानीं सफलं जन्म मन्येऽहं भुवि सत्तमाः ।
आत्मनो द्विजशार्दूला तथा कीर्तिर्यशो बलम् ॥ ९ ॥

Transliteration

idānīṃ saphalaṃ janma manye’haṃ bhuvi sattamāḥ | ātmano dvijaśārdūlā tathā kīrtir yaśo balam || 9 ||

Synonyms

idānīm — now; saphalam janma — fruitful birth; manye aham — I consider; sattamāḥ — excellent ones; dvija-śārdūlāḥ — tigers among twice-born sages; kīrtiḥ, yaśaḥ, balam — fame, renown, strength.

Translation

“Now I consider my birth on earth to be fruitful, O best of men, O foremost of sages; so too my fame, renown, and strength have become meaningful.”

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa 1.1.10

“Blessed and fortunate in merit am I, since you have come to see me—you whose mere remembrance purifies a person.”

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa 1.1.11

Translation

“I wish to hear some unsurpassed teaching of dharma. Out of compassion, O mighty sages, please relate it to me.”

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa 1.1.12

Thus addressed by the king, the brāhmaṇa sages gathered together and deliberated deeply among themselves.

After honouring Vyāsa, they said, “O lord, win Vyāsa’s favour; he will tell this to you.”

ब्रह्मा कुरुकुलश्रेष्ठ शंकराय महात्मने ॥ ९९ ॥

Transliteration

vaitānavratikānāṃ ca tathāsau proktavān vibhuḥ | brahmā kurukulaśreṣṭha śaṃkarāya mahātmane || 99 ||

Synonyms

vaitāna-vratikānām — concerning Vaitāna rites and vows; asau vibhuḥ brahmā — that mighty Brahmā; proktavān — spoke; kuru-kula-śreṣṭha — best of the Kuru line; śaṃkarāya mahātmane — to the great-souled Śaṃkara.

Translation

“And, O best of the Kuru line, mighty Brahmā spoke this teaching, including the Vaitāna rites and vows, to the great-souled Śaṃkara.”

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa 1.1.100

Sanskrit

Transliteration

śaṃkareṇa tathā viṣṇoḥ kathitaṃ kurunandana | viṣṇunāpi punaḥ proktaṃ nāradāya mahīpate || 100 ||

Synonyms

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa 1.1.101

Sanskrit

नारदात्प्राप्तवाञ्छक्रः शक्रादपि पराशरः ।
पराशरात्ततो व्यासो व्यासादपि मया विभो ॥ १०१ ॥

Transliteration

nāradāt prāptavāñ chakraḥ śakrād api parāśaraḥ | parāśarāt tato vyāso vyāsād api mayā vibho || 101 ||

Synonyms

nāradāt — from Nārada; prāptavān śakraḥ — Śakra received it; śakrāt parāśaraḥ — from Śakra, Parāśara; parāśarāt vyāsaḥ — from Parāśara, Vyāsa; vyāsāt mayā — from Vyāsa, by me.

Translation

“Śakra received it from Nārada, Parāśara from Śakra, then Vyāsa from Parāśara, and I received it from Vyāsa, O lord.”

Bhaviṣya Purāṇa 1.1.102
