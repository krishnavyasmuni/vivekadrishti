BP 1.25.1

ब्रह्मोवाच
दक्षिणावर्तलिङ्गश्च नरो वै पुत्रमान्भवेत् ।
वामावर्ते तथा लिङ्गे नरः कन्यां प्रसूयते । । १

Brahmā said: ‘The source says a man whose genital form turns to the right will have a son, while one whose form turns to the left will father a daughter.’

Word-for-word
ब्रह्मा उवाच (brahmā uvāca) — Brahmā said;
दक्षिण-आवर्त-लिङ्गः (dakṣiṇa-āvarta-liṅgaḥ) — genital form turning right;
नरः पुत्रवान् भवेत् (naraḥ putravān bhavet) — man will have son;
वाम-आवर्ते लिङ्गे (vāma-āvarte liṅge) — genital form turning left;
कन्याम् प्रसूयते (kanyām prasūyate) — fathers daughter
Transliteration
brahmovāca
dakṣiṇāvartaliṅgaśca naro vai putramānbhavet |
vāmāvarte tathā liṅge naraḥ kanyāṃ prasūyate | | 1

BP 1.25.2

स्थूलैः शिरालैर्विषमैर्लिङ्गैर्दारिद्र्यमादिशेत् ।
ऋजुभिर्वर्तुलाकारैः पुरुषा पुत्रभागिनः । । २

‘Thick, veiny, or uneven genitalia are traditionally read as indicating poverty; straight and rounded forms are said to indicate sons.’

Word-for-word
स्थूलैः शिरालैः विषमैः लिङ्गैः (sthūlaiḥ śirālaiḥ viṣamaiḥ liṅgaiḥ) — thick veiny uneven genitalia;
दारिद्र्यम् आदिशेत् (dāridryam ādiśet) — predict poverty;
ऋजुभिः वर्तुल-आकारैः (ṛjubhiḥ vartula-ākāraiḥ) — straight rounded;
पुरुषाः पुत्र-भागिनः (puruṣāḥ putra-bhāginaḥ) — men sharing in sons
Transliteration
sthūlaiḥ śirālairviṣamairliṅgairdāridryamādiśet |
ṛjubhirvartulākāraiḥ puruṣā putrabhāginaḥ | | 2

BP 1.25.3

निम्नपादोपविष्टस्य भूमिं स्पृशति मेहनः ।
दुःखितं तं विजानीयात्पुरुषं नात्र संशयः । । ३

‘A seated man whose organ reaches the ground below his feet should, according to the source, be known as distressed—of this there is no doubt.’

Word-for-word
निम्न-पाद-उपविष्टस्य (nimna-pāda-upaviṣṭasya) — of man seated with lowered feet;
भूमिम् स्पृशति मेहनम् (bhūmim spṛśati mehanam) — organ touches earth;
दुःखितम् विजानीयात् (duḥkhitam vijānīyāt) — know him distressed;
न संशयः (na saṃśayaḥ) — no doubt
Transliteration
nimnapādopaviṣṭasya bhūmiṃ spṛśati mehanaḥ |
duḥkhitaṃ taṃ vijānīyātpuruṣaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ | | 3

BP 1.25.4

भूमौ पादोपविष्टस्य गुल्फौ स्पृशति मेहनः ।
ईश्वरं तं विजानीयात्प्रमदानां च वल्लभम्२ । । ४

‘A seated man whose organ reaches his ankles should be known as powerful and beloved by women.’

Word-for-word
भूमौ पाद-उपविष्टस्य (bhūmau pāda-upaviṣṭasya) — seated on ground with feet;
गुल्फौ स्पृशति मेहनम् (gulphau spṛśati mehanam) — organ reaches ankles;
ईश्वरम् विजानीयात् (īśvaram vijānīyāt) — know him powerful;
प्रमदानाम् वल्लभम् (pramadānām vallabham) — beloved of women
Transliteration
bhūmau pādopaviṣṭasya gulphau spṛśati mehanaḥ |
īśvaraṃ taṃ vijānīyātpramadānāṃ ca vallabham2 | | 4

BP 1.25.5

सिंहव्याघ्रसमो यस्य ह्रस्वौ भवति मेहनः ।
भोगवान्स तु विज्ञेयोऽशेषभोगसमन्वितः । । ५

‘One whose organ is short and resembles that of a lion or tiger is traditionally considered prosperous and endowed with every enjoyment.’

Word-for-word
सिंह-व्याघ्र-समः (siṃha-vyāghra-samaḥ) — resembling lion or tiger;
ह्रस्वः मेहनः (hrasvaḥ mehanaḥ) — short organ;
भोगवान् विज्ञेयः (bhogavān vijñeyaḥ) — know as prosperous;
अशेष-भोग-समन्वितः (aśeṣa-bhoga-samanvitaḥ) — endowed with all enjoyments
Transliteration
siṃhavyāghrasamo yasya hrasvau bhavati mehanaḥ |
bhogavānsa tu vijñeyo'śeṣabhogasamanvitaḥ | | 5

BP 1.25.6

रेखाकृतिर्मणिर्यस्य मेहने हि विराजते ।
पार्थिवः स तु विज्ञेयः समुद्रवचनं यथा । । ६

‘According to the Ocean’s teaching, one whose glans displays a line-like formation should be recognized as a ruler.’

Word-for-word
रेखा-आकृतिः (rekhā-ākṛtiḥ) — line-like form;
मणिः यस्य मेहने विराजते (maṇiḥ yasya mehane virājate) — whose glans shines on organ;
पार्थिवः विज्ञेयः (pārthivaḥ vijñeyaḥ) — known as ruler;
समुद्र-वचनम् यथा (samudra-vacanam yathā) — according to Ocean’s words
Transliteration
rekhākṛtirmaṇiryasya mehane hi virājate |
pārthivaḥ sa tu vijñeyaḥ samudravacanaṃ yathā | | 6

BP 1.25.7

सुवर्णरजतप्रख्यैर्मणिमुक्तासमप्रभैः ।
प्रवालसदृशैः स्निग्धैर्मणिभिः पार्थिवो भवेत् । । ७

‘The source says one becomes a ruler when the glans is smooth and resembles gold, silver, jewels, pearls, or coral in brilliance.’

Word-for-word
सुवर्ण-रजत-प्रख्यैः (suvarṇa-rajata-prakhyaiḥ) — resembling gold and silver;
मणि-मुक्ता-सम-प्रभैः (maṇi-muktā-sama-prabhaiḥ) — brilliant like jewels and pearls;
प्रवाल-सदृशैः (pravāla-sadṛśaiḥ) — like coral;
स्निग्धैः मणिभिः (snigdhaiḥ maṇibhiḥ) — smooth glans;
पार्थिवः भवेत् (pārthivaḥ bhavet) — becomes ruler
Transliteration
suvarṇarajataprakhyairmaṇimuktāsamaprabhaiḥ |
pravālasadṛśaiḥ snigdhairmaṇibhiḥ pārthivo bhavet | | 7

BP 1.25.8

पाण्डुरैर्मलिनै रूक्षैर्दीर्घव्यासैर्दिशो व्रजेत् ।
समैस्तथोन्नतैश्चापि सुस्निग्धैर्मणिभिर्गृही । । ८

‘Pale, dirty, rough, elongated forms are traditionally associated with wandering the regions; even, raised, and very smooth forms with being a householder.’

Word-for-word
पाण्डुरैः मलिनैः रूक्षैः (pāṇḍuraiḥ malinaiḥ rūkṣaiḥ) — pale dirty rough;
दीर्घ-व्यासैः (dīrgha-vyāsaiḥ) — elongated;
दिशः व्रजेत् (diśaḥ vrajet) — wanders directions;
समैः उन्नतैः सु-स्निग्धैः (samaiḥ unnataiḥ su-snigdhaiḥ) — even raised very smooth;
गृही (gṛhī) — householder
Transliteration
pāṇḍurairmalinai rūkṣairdīrghavyāsairdiśo vrajet |
samaistathonnataiścāpi susnigdhairmaṇibhirgṛhī | | 8

BP 1.25.9

धनरक्षास्तथा स्त्रीणां भोक्तारस्ते भवन्ति हि ।
मणिभिर्मध्यनिम्नैस्तु पितरस्ते भवन्ति हि । । ९

‘The source says such men preserve wealth and enjoy women, while those with a depression at the centre of the glans become fathers.’

Word-for-word
धन-रक्षाः (dhana-rakṣāḥ) — protectors of wealth;
स्त्रीणाम् भोक्तारः (strīṇām bhoktāraḥ) — enjoyers of women;
मणिभिः मध्य-निम्नैः (maṇibhiḥ madhya-nimnaiḥ) — with centrally depressed glans;
पितरः भवन्ति (pitaraḥ bhavanti) — become fathers
Transliteration
dhanarakṣāstathā strīṇāṃ bhoktāraste bhavanti hi |
maṇibhirmadhyanimnaistu pitaraste bhavanti hi | | 9

BP 1.25.10

युवतीनां महाबाहो निःस्वाश्चापि भवन्ति ते ।
नोल्बणैश्चापि धनिनो नरा वीरा भवन्ति हि । । 1.25.१०

‘Mighty-armed one, the text says some become penniless in relation to young women, whereas men without excessive prominence become wealthy and heroic.’

Word-for-word
युवतीनाम् (yuvatīnām) — in relation to young women;
निःस्वाः भवन्ति (niḥsvāḥ bhavanti) — become penniless;
न उल्बणैः (na ulbaṇaiḥ) — with forms not excessive;
धनिनः नराः (dhaninaḥ narāḥ) — wealthy men;
वीराः भवन्ति (vīrāḥ bhavanti) — become heroic
Transliteration
yuvatīnāṃ mahābāho niḥsvāścāpi bhavanti te |
nolbaṇaiścāpi dhanino narā vīrā bhavanti hi | | 1.25.10

BP 1.25.11

मूत्रधारा पतेदेका वलिता दक्षिणा यदि ।
स भवेत्पार्थिवः पृथ्व्याः समुद्रवचनं यथा । । ११

‘According to the Ocean, if urine falls in a single stream curving to the right, the man will become a ruler of the earth.’

Word-for-word
मूत्र-धारा पतेत् एका (mūtra-dhārā patet ekā) — urine falls in one stream;
वलिता दक्षिणा (valitā dakṣiṇā) — curving right;
सः भवेत् पार्थिवः पृथ्व्याः (saḥ bhavet pārthivaḥ pṛthvyāḥ) — becomes ruler of earth;
समुद्र-वचनम् यथा (samudra-vacanam yathā) — according to Ocean
Transliteration
mūtradhārā patedekā valitā dakṣiṇā yadi |
sa bhavetpārthivaḥ pṛthvyāḥ samudravacanaṃ yathā | | 11

BP 1.25.12

द्वे धारे च तथा स्निग्धे धनवान्भोगवान्स्मृतः ।
बहुधारास्तथा रूक्षाः सशब्दाः पुरुषाधमाः । । १२

‘Two smooth streams are traditionally associated with wealth and enjoyment; numerous rough and noisy streams with an inferior condition.’

Word-for-word

धनवान् भोगवान् स्मृतः (dhanavān bhogavān smṛtaḥ) — remembered wealthy and prosperous;
बहु-धाराः रूक्षाः स-शब्दाः (bahu-dhārāḥ rūkṣāḥ sa-śabdāḥ) — many rough noisy streams;
पुरुष-अधमाः (puruṣa-adhamāḥ) — inferior men
Transliteration
dve dhāre ca tathā snigdhe dhanavānbhogavānsmṛtaḥ |

BP 1.25.13

BP 1.25.14

१मूषगन्धिर्भवेत्पुत्री पद्मगन्धिर्नृपः स्मृतः ।

BP 1.25.15
