Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam — Canto 10

The Summum Bonum

Prabhupāda’s Bhāgavatam text with Śrīdhara Svāmī’s Bhāvārtha-dīpikā.

Chapter 1

10.1 The Advent of Lord Kṛṣṇa: Introduction

ŚB 10.1.1

श्रीराजोवाच
कथितो वंशविस्तारो भवता सोमसूर्ययोः ।
राज्ञां चोभयवंश्यानां चरितं परमाद्भुतम् ॥ १ ॥

King Parīkṣit said: My dear lord, you have elaborately described the dynasties of both the moon-god and the sun-god, with the exalted and wonderful character of their kings.

Word-for-word

śrī-rājā uvāca — King Parīkṣit said; kathitaḥ — has already been described; vaṁśa-vistāraḥ — a broad description of the dynasties; bhavatā — by Your Lordship; soma-sūryayoḥ — of the moon-god and the sun-god; rājñām — of the kings; ca — and; ubhaya — both; vaṁśyānām — of the members of the dynasties; caritam — the character; parama — exalted; adbhutam — and wonderful.

Transliteration
śrī-rājovāca
kathito vaṁśa-vistāro
bhavatā soma-sūryayoḥ
rājñāṁ cobhaya-vaṁśyānāṁ
caritaṁ paramādbhutam
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

śrīmad-bhāgavatasya daśame skandhe āśraya-saṁjñitasya śrī-kṛṣṇasya līlā-varṇanārthaṁ ṣaṣṭhādi-skandheṣu pañcabhir nirūpitasya manvantareśānukathā-lakṣaṇasyaiśa-padārthasyādhikārārtham antaritaṁ yat tad-vaṁśānucaritaṁ saṅkṣipya pūrayiṣyan rājā pṛcchati—kathita iti dvābhyām || 1 ||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. śrīmad-bhāgavatasya — of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam; daśame skandhe — in the Tenth Canto; āśraya-saṁjñitasya — designated as “the shelter”; śrī-kṛṣṇasya — of Śrī Kṛṣṇa; līlā-varṇanārtham — for the purpose of describing the pastimes; ṣaṣṭhādi-skandheṣu pañcabhiḥ — in the five cantos beginning with the Sixth; nirūpitasya — described; manvantareśānukathā-lakṣaṇasya — characterized by narration of the Lord through the manvantaras; aiśa-padārthasya — of the subject denoted by “īśa”; adhikārārtham — for establishing the topic; antaritaṁ yat — what had been interrupted; tad-vaṁśānucaritam — that account of the dynasty; saṅkṣipya — summarizing; pūrayiṣyan — intending to complete; rājā pṛcchati — the King asks; kathita iti dvābhyām — in the two verses beginning with “kathita.”

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. In the Tenth Canto of the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, in order to describe the pastimes of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, who is designated as the āśraya, or shelter, the account of the dynasties that had been interrupted is now briefly completed so that the subject may be properly established. Therefore the King asks in the two verses beginning with “kathita.”

ŚB 10.1.2

यदोश्च धर्मशीलस्य नितरां मुनिसत्तम ।
तत्रांशेनावतीर्णस्य विष्णोर्वीर्याणि शंस नः ॥ २ ॥

O best of munis, you have also described the descendants of Yadu, who were very pious and strictly adherent to religious principles. Now, if you will, kindly describe the wonderful, glorious activities of Lord Viṣṇu, or Kṛṣṇa, who appeared in that Yadu dynasty with Baladeva, His plenary expansion.

Word-for-word

yadoḥ — of Yadu or the Yadu dynasty; ca — also; dharma-śīlasya — who were strictly attached to religious principles; nitarām — highly qualified; muni-sattama — O best of all munis, king of the munis (Śukadeva Gosvāmī); tatra — in that dynasty; aṁśena — with His plenary expansion Baladeva; avatīrṇasya — who appeared as an incarnation; viṣṇoḥ — of Lord Viṣṇu; vīryāṇi — the glorious activities; śaṁsa — kindly describe; naḥ — unto us.

Transliteration
yadoś ca dharma-śīlasya
nitarāṁ muni-sattama
tatrāṁśenāvatīrṇasya
viṣṇor vīryāṇi śaṁsa naḥ
Śrīdhara Sanskrit

aṁśeneti pratīty abhiprāyeṇoktam || 2 ||

Śrīdhara word-for-word. aṁśena iti — the expression “aṁśena”; pratītya — according to an understood sense; abhiprāyeṇa — with the intended purport; uktam — is stated.

Śrīdhara’s Commentary. Śrīdhara notes that the word aṁśena is stated according to the intended understanding of the passage.
