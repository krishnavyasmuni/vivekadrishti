BP 1.20.1

शतानीक उवाच
ब्रूहि मे द्विजशार्दूल द्वितीयां फलसंज्ञिताम् ।
यामुपोष्य नरो योषिद्वियोगं नेह गच्छति । । १

Śatānīka said: ‘Tiger among brāhmaṇas, tell me of the second lunar day called Phala, by fasting on which neither man nor woman suffers separation here.’

Word-for-word
शतानीकः उवाच (śatānīkaḥ uvāca) — Śatānīka said;
ब्रूहि मे (brūhi me) — tell me;
द्विज-शार्दूल (dvija-śārdūla) — tiger among brāhmaṇas;
द्वितीयाम् फल-संज्ञिताम् (dvitīyām phala-saṃjñitām) — second day named Phala;
याम् उपोष्य (yām upoṣya) — fasting on which;
नरः योषित् (naraḥ yoṣit) — man or woman;
वियोगम् न गच्छति (viyogam na gacchati) — does not undergo separation
Transliteration
śatānīka uvāca
brūhi me dvijaśārdūla dvitīyāṃ phalasaṃjñitām |
yāmupoṣya naro yoṣidviyogaṃ neha gacchati | | 1

BP 1.20.2

पत्न्या नरो मुनिश्रेष्ठ भार्या च पतिना १ सह ।
तामहं श्रोतुमिच्छामि विधवा स्त्री न जायते । ।
उपोषितेन येनार्य पत्न्या च सहितो नरः । । २

‘O best sage, a man remains with his wife and a wife with her husband; a woman does not become widowed when she and her husband observe this fast. I wish to hear of it.’

Word-for-word
पत्न्या नरः (patnyā naraḥ) — man with wife;
भार्या पतिना सह (bhāryā patinā saha) — wife with husband;
श्रोतुम् इच्छामि (śrotum icchāmi) — I wish to hear;
विधवा स्त्री न जायते (vidhavā strī na jāyate) — woman does not become widow;
उपोषितेन येन (upoṣitena yena) — by which fast;
पत्न्या सहितः नरः (patnyā sahitaḥ naraḥ) — man together with wife
Transliteration
patnyā naro muniśreṣṭha bhāryā ca patinā 1 saha |
tāmahaṃ śrotumicchāmi vidhavā strī na jāyate | |
upoṣitena yenārya patnyā ca sahito naraḥ | | 2

BP 1.20.3

श्रेयः-अर्थम् (śreyaḥ-artham) — for welfare;
नर-योषिताम् (nara-yoṣitām) — of men and women;
कौतुकम् (kautukam) — curiosity;
श्रुत्वा अपूर्वम् (śrutvā apūrvam) — hearing unprecedented;

Transliteration

BP 1.20.4

सुमन्तुरुवाच
अशून्यशयनां नाम द्वितीयां शृणु भारत ।
यामुपोष्य न वैधव्यं स्त्री प्रयाति नराधिप । ।
पत्नीवियुक्तश्च नरो न कदाचित्प्रजायते । ।४

Sumantu said: ‘O Bharata, hear of the second day named Aśūnya-śayana. A woman who fasts on it does not become widowed, and a man is never separated from his wife.’

Word-for-word
सुमन्तुः उवाच (sumantuḥ uvāca) — Sumantu said;
अशून्य-शयनाम् नाम द्वितीयाम् (aśūnya-śayanām nāma dvitīyām) — second day named Aśūnya-śayana;
शृणु भारत (śṛṇu bhārata) — hear O Bharata;
याम् उपोष्य (yām upoṣya) — fasting on which;
न वैधव्यम् स्त्री प्रयाति (na vaidhavyam strī prayāti) — woman does not attain widowhood;
पत्नी-वियुक्तः नरः न (patnī-viyuktaḥ naraḥ na) — man not separated from wife
Transliteration
sumanturuvāca
aśūnyaśayanāṃ nāma dvitīyāṃ śṛṇu bhārata |
yāmupoṣya na vaidhavyaṃ strī prayāti narādhipa | |
patnīviyuktaśca naro na kadācitprajāyate | |4

BP 1.20.5

शेते जगत्पतिः कृष्णः श्रिया सार्धं यदा नृप ।
अशून्यशयना नाम तदा ग्राह्या हि सा तिथिः । । ५

‘When Kṛṣṇa, Lord of the world, lies down together with Śrī, O king, that lunar date should be observed as Aśūnya-śayana, the “bed not left empty.”’

Word-for-word
शेते जगत्-पतिः कृष्णः (śete jagat-patiḥ kṛṣṇaḥ) — Kṛṣṇa lord of world lies;
श्रिया सार्धम् (śriyā sārdham) — together with Śrī;
यदा (yadā) — when;
अशून्य-शयना नाम (aśūnya-śayanā nāma) — named non-empty bed;
तदा ग्राह्या सा तिथिः (tadā grāhyā sā tithiḥ) — then that date is to be observed
Transliteration
śete jagatpatiḥ kṛṣṇaḥ śriyā sārdhaṃ yadā nṛpa |
aśūnyaśayanā nāma tadā grāhyā hi sā tithiḥ | | 5

BP 1.20.6

कृष्णपक्षे द्वितीयायां श्रावणे मासि भारत ।
इदमुच्चारयेत्स्नातः प्रणम्य जगतः पतिम् । ।
श्रीवत्सधारिणं देवं भक्त्याभ्यर्च्य श्रिया सह । । ६

‘On the second day of the dark fortnight in Śrāvaṇa, one should bathe, bow to the Lord of the world, worship with devotion the god bearing Śrīvatsa together with Śrī, and recite this prayer.’

Word-for-word
कृष्ण-पक्षे द्वितीयायाम् (kṛṣṇa-pakṣe dvitīyāyām) — dark fortnight second day;
श्रावणे मासि (śrāvaṇe māsi) — in Śrāvaṇa;
स्नातः (snātaḥ) — bathed;
प्रणम्य जगतः पतिम् (praṇamya jagataḥ patim) — bowing to world’s lord;
श्रीवत्स-धारिणम् देवम् (śrīvatsa-dhāriṇam devam) — god bearing Śrīvatsa;
भक्त्या अभ्यर्च्य श्रिया सह (bhaktyā abhyarcya śriyā saha) — worshipping with devotion together with Śrī;
इदम् उच्चारयेत् (idam uccārayet) — recite this
Transliteration
kṛṣṇapakṣe dvitīyāyāṃ śrāvaṇe māsi bhārata |
idamuccārayetsnātaḥ praṇamya jagataḥ patim | |
śrīvatsadhāriṇaṃ devaṃ bhaktyābhyarcya śriyā saha | | 6

BP 1.20.7

श्रीवत्सधारिञ्छ्रीकान्त श्रीवत्स श्रीपतेऽव्यय ।
गार्हस्थ्यं मा प्रणाशं मे यातु धर्मार्थकामदम् । । ७

‘“Bearer of Śrīvatsa, beloved of Śrī, imperishable Lord of Śrī: may my household life, which bestows righteousness, prosperity, and pleasure, never perish.”’

Word-for-word
श्रीवत्स-धारिन् (śrīvatsa-dhārin) — bearer of Śrīvatsa;
श्री-कान्त (śrī-kānta) — beloved of Śrī;
श्री-पते अव्यय (śrī-pate avyaya) — imperishable Lord of Śrī;
गार्हस्थ्यम् मे (gārhasthyam me) — my household life;
मा प्रणाशम् यातु (mā praṇāśam yātu) — may it not perish;
धर्म-अर्थ-काम-दम् (dharma-artha-kāma-dam) — giving righteousness wealth pleasure
Transliteration
śrīvatsadhāriñchrīkānta śrīvatsa śrīpate'vyaya |
gārhasthyaṃ mā praṇāśaṃ me yātu dharmārthakāmadam | | 7

BP 1.20.8

गावश्च मा प्रणश्यन्तु मा प्रणश्यन्तु मे जनाः । । ८

‘“May my cattle not perish, and may my people not perish.”’

‘“May my family relations not be destroyed through marital division arising from me. God, just as you are never separated from Lakṣmī, may I never be separated from prosperity.”’
