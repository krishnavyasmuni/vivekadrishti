  • Chapter 38 — The Āśvayuja Nāga-pañcamī Vow

BP 1.38.1

॥ ॥ सुमंतुरुवाच ॥ ॥
तथा चाश्वयुजे मासि पञ्चम्यां कुरुनंदन ॥
कृत्वा कुशमयान्नागान्गंधांद्यैः संप्रपूजयेत् ॥ १ ॥

Sumantu said: Likewise, descendant of Kuru, on Pañcamī in the month of Āśvayuja one should fashion Nāgas from kuśa grass and worship them thoroughly with fragrances and other offerings.

BP 1.38.2

घृतोदकाभ्यां पयसा स्नपयित्वा विशांपते ॥
गोधूमैः पयसा स्विन्नैर्भक्ष्यैश्च विविधैस्तथा ॥ २ ॥

Ruler of the people, after bathing them with ghee-water and milk, one should offer wheat cooked in milk and various foods.

BP 1.38.3

यस्त्वस्यां विधिवन्नागाञ्छुचिर्भक्त्या समन्वितः ॥
पूजयेत्कुरुशार्दूल तस्य शेषादयो नृप ॥३॥

Tiger of the Kuru line, when a pure and devoted person duly worships the Nāgas on this day, Śeṣa and the other Nāgas become pleased with that person.

BP 1.38.4

नागाः प्रीता भवंतीह शांतिमाप्नोति वा विभो ॥
स शांतिलोकमासाद्य मोदते शाश्वतीः समाः ॥ ४ ॥

Lord, the pleased Nāgas grant peace here. Reaching the world of peace, that person delights for eternal years.

BP 1.38.9

इत्येष कथितो वीर पञ्चमीकल्प उत्तमः ।
यत्रायमुच्यते मंत्रः सर्वसर्पनिषेधकः ॥ ९॥
ॐ कुरुकुल्ले फट् स्वाहा।॥

Hero, thus has been told the excellent Pañcamī procedure, in which this mantra that wards off all snakes is spoken: ‘Oṃ Kurukulle phaṭ svāhā.’ The Sanskrit witness numbers this concluding passage as verse 9 and contains no transmitted verses 5–8.
